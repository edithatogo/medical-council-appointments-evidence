# Rights

Original research annotations: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Project-owned verification code: Apache License 2.0, see LICENSE-CODE. Neither licence grants rights over source documents or externally quoted text.

Three official New Zealand Supreme Court judgments are included under the identified Copyright Act 1994 section 27 basis. Preserve their original notices and any independent publication restrictions. They are not Apache-licensed. See data/redistributed-files.json for exact files, hashes, original URLs and bounded checks.

All 119 public source groups have individual entries in data/source-rights.json. Most are represented through original metadata, URLs and version hashes rather than complete copies. Missing positive permission is not a finding that every possible reproduction is prohibited. The central Ministry bundle remains linked and catalogued, with mixed-content limitations explained in data/central-source-availability.json. Private correspondence is excluded.

Five text derivatives are included under the individual bases and selection limits in data/text-derivatives.json. Four contain specified legislative text; the Smylie article derivative carries its article-specific CC BY 4.0 attribution. Original publisher website wrappers, images and external supplementary files are not reproduced.
