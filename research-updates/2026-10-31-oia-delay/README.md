# Timely access and the September release

As at 31 October 2026, the APH appointments paper has been located in the September release. The minute APH-26-MIN-0074 and Love-specific reasons remain unlocated. Later references to the minute establish its identifier, not its contents.

The public request in FYI34918 expressly included Cabinet committee minutes, decisions and APH recommendations. The Ministerial response SBOIA26-1155 applied section18(d) to appointment information because it would be published by mid-September. It expressly named the appointments paper, without individually naming the minute. The missing minute raises a question about the completeness and scope of that promised release. No document-specific disposition for the minute was identified in the examined material.

The Ombudsman guide updated May2026 says the requested information itself must be available; publishing related information is insufficient. If only part is to be published, the remaining information still needs a decision. It recognises that urgency for informed participation can make waiting unreasonable. A complaint can challenge a refusal immediately; the requester need not await the promised publication date.

The consequence is practical as well as procedural. Refusal pending publication postpones direct access; an incomplete release leaves the requester pursuing the gap again. By31October, advance voting had begun on26October, with election day7November. Later disclosure or review cannot restore an opportunity to use the information before a vote already cast. This is an inference about timely accountability, not proof of electoral motive or a prediction that an Ombudsman could not act urgently.

Contrary evidence matters. In Treasury case013497,12–15weeks was accepted because of a complex416-document Budget release. There is no universal fixed time limit. In the separate Ministry of Health DHB-data case463213, foreseeable election-related ministerial input undermined certainty of publication; that ruling does not determine the legality of these appointment refusals.

The specific next evidential step is to seek the minute and its release status: supply it, identify the applicable basis for withholding, or clarify where and when it was published, and explain its absence from the located bundle. No external request was sent during this research update.
